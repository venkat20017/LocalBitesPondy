FAQ

Questions IT Ops Managers always ask.

Does FlowPilot AI replace my existing ITSM tool?
▾
No. FlowPilot AI is an augmentation layer, not a replacement. It sits between your email/inbox and your existing Freshservice, Jira, or ServiceNow instance — making the triage decisions those tools expect humans to make. Your current tool, your current data, your current workflows stay intact.
How long does it take to set up?
▾
Connection takes approximately 30 minutes via API. FlowPilot AI then reads your historical ticket data and builds your environment's classification model over 48 hours. By Day 3, automation is running. No consultants. No infrastructure changes. No implementation project plan.
What if our ticket patterns are unique to our industry?
▾
FlowPilot AI doesn't use generic templates. It learns from your historical tickets specifically — your team names, your category structures, your priority conventions. The model is built for your environment, not a hypothetical average IT team.
How do I justify this to my CTO?
▾
That's exactly what the free checklist helps you do. It walks you through calculating your team's current triage cost in hours and dollars, then maps those savings against the investment. Most IT Ops Managers find their team loses 8–15 hours per engineer per week to manual admin — that number, translated to salary cost, is usually the business case.
We're a small team. Is FlowPilot AI overkill for us?
▾
Actually, small teams benefit the most. A 4-person IT team losing 2 hours per person per day to manual triage is losing 40% of their total capacity to admin work. Automation doesn't just help at scale — it's most impactful when there's no slack capacity to absorb the manual work.
