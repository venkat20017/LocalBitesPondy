Overview

FlowPilot AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information you provide when you visit our website or download our resources.

This policy applies to information collected through our website, including our lead magnet download forms, blog content, and any future product features.

The short version: We collect only what we need to deliver the resource you requested. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

📥
What We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a form on our website to download the IT Triage Automation Checklist, we collect:

  • First name
  • Work email address
  • Company name
  • Current ITSM tool (e.g. Freshservice, Jira, ServiceNow)
  • IT team size

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL (how you found us)
  • General geographic location (country/city level only)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This information is collected through Google Analytics 4 and is used only to understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.

⚙️
How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To deliver the resource you requested (the IT Triage Automation Checklist)
  • To send follow-up emails related to IT operations and automation — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • To understand which ITSM tools and team sizes are most represented in our audience
  • To improve our content, resources, and future product offerings
  • To respond to any questions or feedback you send us directly
We will never: sell your information to third parties, use your email for unrelated commercial advertising, or share your company details with competitors or data brokers.

🗄️
Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard tools. Form submissions are processed through Formspree and stored in our email management platform (HubSpot). Both platforms use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We do not store payment information as we do not currently charge for any resources on this website.

🤝
Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Tools like Formspree (form processing), HubSpot (email delivery), and Google Analytics (website analytics) that process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business transfer: In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, your data may transfer to the new entity under the same privacy commitments

Each third-party service provider we use is bound by their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them: Formspree, HubSpot, Google.

🍪
Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): To understand how visitors use our site. Data is anonymized and aggregated.
  • Functional cookies: To remember your preferences and improve your experience on return visits.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit some functionality of the website. We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies at this time.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data from our systems
  • Unsubscribe: Opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address listed in the Contact section below. We will respond within 30 days.

⏱️
Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Email subscribers: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Analytics data: Retained for 14 months per Google Analytics default settings
  • Form submissions: Retained for up to 2 years unless deletion is requested earlier

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

Children's Privacy

FlowPilot AI is a B2B platform designed for IT operations professionals. Our website and resources are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
